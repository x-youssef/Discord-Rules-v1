Server Rules

Follow these rules to keep our community safe, fair, and enjoyable for everyone.
1
Be Respectful
No harassment, hate speech, racism, or discrimination of any kind. Treat every member with respect.
2
No NSFW Content
No explicit, violent, or inappropriate content anywhere in the server, including profile pictures and statuses.
3
No Spamming
No repeated messages, excessive caps, emoji spam, or mass mentions of members and roles.
4
No Advertising
No promoting other servers, links, or products without staff permission. DM advertising is also not allowed.
5
No Impersonation
Don't pretend to be staff, other members, bots, or public figures. Usernames should represent you honestly.
6
Use Channels Properly
Post content in the correct channel — off-topic messages will be removed or moved by staff.
7
No Harmful Content
No malware, scams, phishing links, IP grabbers, or NSFW/gore material of any kind.
8
English Only
Keep main channels in English so everyone in the community can understand and take part.
9
Follow Discord ToS
All Discord Terms of Service and Community Guidelines apply here, without exception.
10
Listen to Staff
Staff decisions are final. If you disagree, DM a moderator respectfully instead of arguing publicly.
⚠️
Violations result in warnings, timeouts, kicks, or bans depending on severity and repetition.
